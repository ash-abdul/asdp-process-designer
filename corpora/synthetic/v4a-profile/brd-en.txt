# Trade Licence Renewal — Business Requirements

## 1. Purpose
This document describes the renewal of a commercial trade licence for an
establishment already registered with the authority.

## 2. Scope
Renewal only. New registrations and cancellations are out of scope.

## 3. Eligibility
An establishment may renew when its licence expires within ninety days, its
registration is active, and no unpaid administrative fine is outstanding.

## 4. Required documents
| Document | Condition |
|---|---|
| Tenancy contract | Required when the registered address changed |
| Authorised signatory letter | Required when the applicant is not the owner |

## 5. Fees
A renewal fee applies per licensed activity. A late renewal adds a monthly
surcharge for each month after expiry.

## 6. Decision
An application is approved when eligibility holds and documents are complete;
returned for correction when a document is missing; and refused when the
establishment's registration has lapsed for more than one year.

# Trade Licence Renewal

## 1. Eligibility
An establishment must hold an active registration to renew its licence.
The applicant must submit the renewal request within ninety days of expiry.

## 2. Documents
A tenancy contract is required when the registered address has changed.
The applicant must supply a valid identity document.

## 3. Review
The reviewing officer must complete the review within three working days.
An application is refused when the registration has lapsed for more than one year.

## 4. Fees
A late renewal surcharge applies for each month after expiry.
